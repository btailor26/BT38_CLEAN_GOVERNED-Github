# Product image orientation and fidelity gate

Applies to new and refreshed listings. Specification only; automated QA is not yet implemented.

1. Identify product and intended use before choosing viewing angle.
2. Main image: show entire product in natural front-facing orientation where appropriate, with correct drape, no distortion or misleading cropping.
3. Preserve original product: bead count and order, colours, shape, pendant, fastening, proportions and included parts. No invented or missing components.
4. Secondary images: realistic use angle, close-up detail and verified measurement view; obey each marketplace's rules.
5. Deity mala: front view must show full ribbon, beads and hanging pendant; use image must show realistic fit on an appropriate idol without unsupported size claims.
6. Dimension arrows only after measurement endpoints are verified.
7. Compare generated images against original source; reject differences and regenerate before seller approval.
8. Record approval and rejected versions. Never publish unapproved imagery.
